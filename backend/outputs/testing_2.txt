**Protection of Civilians under International Humanitarian Law**

IHL provides a framework for the protection of civilians in armed conflict. The principle of distinction, enshrined in Article 51 of Additional Protocol I, obliges parties to distinguish between civilians and combatants, as well as between civilian objects and military objectives. This principle is further reinforced by the principle of proportionality, which requires that the harm caused to civilians be proportionate to the military advantage anticipated.
